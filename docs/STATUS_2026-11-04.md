## 2. What TMEM114 turned out to be
- It is NOT an ordinary read-closable N gap. In the deposited hap1 (GCA_982266845.1) the gene locus
  `OZ418350.1:111281769-111285248` is 3.5 kb, while the betulina gene locus is 28.1 kb (4 exons).
  The 70 kb missing segment is not covered by a 100-N scaffolding gap.
- Reads on hap1: 15 spanning reads support the deleted version.
- Deposited hap2 carries the whole gene, 4/4 exons, at `OZ418090.1:111009554-111039906`
  (the 86,045 bp gene allele with its 65 kb insert is identical on hap2, `OZ418090.1:110279645-110365690`).
- Call: **heterozygous deletion. The gene is present in the animal (on hap2).** hap1 holds the deleted allele.
  Source rows: `therioserver:/home/toki/gof/geneloss2/final.tsv` (TMEM114_52 and SMCO1_12).
- SMCO1 is the same pattern: hap1 has exon 1 only (1/3), hap2 has all 3 exons.
- Reads cannot repair this on hap1, because the sequence is absent from that allele. There is nothing to fill.

I could not tie the owner's exact "N region" to a single record, so if the NNN they meant was a different
stretch, give me its coordinates and I will trace it.
